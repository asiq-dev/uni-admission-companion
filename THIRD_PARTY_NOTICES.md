All dependencies are bundled locally. No CDN or external API is used at runtime.

See [SUBJECT_COVERAGE.md](SUBJECT_COVERAGE.md) for pinned revisions, exact counts, exclusions and reuse details. `js/services/subject-notices.js` accompanies exports and full backups; all records retain attribution and adaptation/verification labels.

Keep attribution, license links, change notices and applicable noncommercial/share-alike terms with copies and derivatives. ## English vocabulary and geography data

The **2,000-question English vocabulary supplement** adapts definitions and lexical relations from **WordNet 3.0, Copyright 2006 Princeton University**, archived in `data/source-material/wordnet.zip` from the NLTK data mirror. The full permission, copyright and warranty notice is reproduced in `data/source-material/wordnet-LICENSE.txt` and `js/services/bank-notices.js`. Adaptations are definition-to-word templates, sense selection, randomized distractors and explanation comparisons. [WordNet license and terms](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use). No endorsement is implied. Preserve the full notice when copying the data or adaptations.

The **945-question general knowledge supplement** adapts geographical facts from **mledoze/countries and contributors**, commit `fa4f62182d19ff4f8b325cde6387c9cd9d80955a`, under **[ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)**. Attribution: [Countries dataset](https://github.com/mledoze/countries). **The adapted geography question database is available under ODbL 1.0 too.** Changes: field selection, conservative exclusion rules, factual question templates, distractors and source-context explanations. Full original source, modification script, audit and machine-readable adapted database are bundled; see `data/source-material/countries-countries.json`, `data/source-material/countries-LICENSE`, `tools/build_english_gk_banks.py` and `data/general-knowledge.json`. These terms apply to this derived database; unrelated collections and app code retain their own terms.

Both collections are supplementary practice, not university past papers. Full backups and app question-bank exports containing these collections include license notices, in addition to per-question attribution and verification labels. Share the complete export wrapper, or accompany extracted records with the applicable full notices and access to the original/modified database and conversion script. License notices do not establish independent factual or pedagogical verification.

## Selected question excerpts

The additional `data/satt-humanities.json` contains **15 limited short excerpts** from individually attributed public Satt Academy question pages (JU 3 / GST 9 / NU 3). Each included stem/options excerpt is at most 22 whitespace-delimited words. Correct source options are retained and explanations are original Admission Companion editorial. The 30-page fixed selection and all exclusions are recorded in its audit; no full bank, source explanation, paid content or authenticated original university paper is represented as copied here. The source field explicitly states the verification and limited-excerpt status. This does **not** claim an open licence or blanket redistribution permission for Satt Academy content. Source rights remain with their respective owners. Personal/noncommercial intent alone is not represented as such permission.

The optional sourced starter pack uses short, attributed question/option excerpts from individual public Satt Academy pages. Each record contains its source URL; the audit is in data/sourced-practice-audit.json. This does not claim an open license for Satt Academy or redistribute a complete website question bank. Original source rights remain with their owners. Explanations in this pack were written for Admission Companion and are labelled editorial, not official university keys or copied source explanations. Historical university/year claims have not been independently authenticated.

Attribution and change notices persist in each question's source field and the offline attribution file.
