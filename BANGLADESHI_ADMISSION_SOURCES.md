# Bangladeshi Humanities admission sources

Checked 17 November 2026. This shortlist prioritises Bangladeshi admission teaching and question banks for JU, GST and NU. It records what publishers publicly list; it does not certify their teaching quality, question accuracy, historical examination claims, current eligibility rules or redistribution rights. Courses can change. No enrolment, payment, login, message to a publisher or new question import was performed during this review. Existing student data and banks remain unchanged.

## Important correction: the supplied NU university-hosted PDF

The supplied [university-hosted Bachelor of Arts sample](https://www.nu.ac.bd/Syllabus/Syllabus_Honours/Sample_Questions/Sample_Question_%28Session_2024-25%29_Bachelor_of_Arts.pdf) was successfully downloaded and inspected. It has **21 pages**. The cover identifies four-year B.A. Honours programme samples; the English page identifies Honours First Year, a course subject code, 80 marks and a four-hour written examination. This is **not an admission-MCQ sample paper**. Do not import it as NU admission questions or use its marks/time as an admission pattern. It is retained only as source-review evidence at `data/source-material/nu-official-arts-sample.pdf`; cover and English page were rendered for visual checking.

## Offline reuse and access limits

- [Satt Academy terms](https://sattacademy.com/terms-and-conditions) reserve content rights and restrict making new works from it; the terms also discuss attributed sharing. That is not a clear bulk offline-database licence. No bulk extraction was performed during this review.
- [Somadhan terms](https://somadhan.app/terms) explicitly prohibit scrapers/bots and restrict copying/republication; answers are login-gated on the reviewed public pages. No gate was bypassed and no answer was guessed to fill the missing key.
- [Practiceclub terms](https://www.practiceclub.net/fterms-of-uses) require written consent for reproduction/distribution/modification. Personal access is not a bulk-import licence.
- [Fahim's GK terms](https://fahimsgk.com/terms) describe personal access and prohibit sharing exam content/redistributing paid material. No question-bank export permission was established.
- Public PDF download buttons or course enrolment do not, by themselves, establish permission to reproduce a publisher's complete question bank in this app. Use an authorised export or permission covering the intended offline reuse. User-supplied study documents can be reviewed for conversion, retaining attribution and clear verification labels; missing keys or ambiguous scans must be resolved before practice import.

The existing bank already contains Bangla/BD collections and a small attributed Satt starter pack, but its broad English/science/US-focused supplements are not a substitute for target-specific Bangladeshi admission material. Those older optional records have **not** been removed, relabelled as local past papers or silently replaced. No new authenticated-question count is claimed from this research.

## Collection contract for the next usable local bank

Prefer Humanities questions and retain Bangla wording, all four options, the source answer, source rationale (if supplied), exact paper/session/unit/set/question number and source URL. Verify paper identity separately from answer correctness. Distinguish original university paper, publisher's past-paper claim, source-key-only item and editorial explanation. Use one record with explicit preparation links when a question serves multiple targets. Keep unresolved or conflicting keys out of practice. Import through the existing append/deduplication workflow, then test target/subject filters, random practice and model tests without altering previous attempts/bookmarks/history.
